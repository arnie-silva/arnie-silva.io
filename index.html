Arnaldo (Arnie) Silva

Electrical and Computer Engineer

 As a lifelong enthusiast in technology, it has been my ultimate goal to build functional applications designed beautifully. I want to enrich lives through my work and passions whilst leaving the world better than I found it. I want to be at the forefront of integrating new technologies in products to enhance how we all experience life through the tools developers have built.
 In my current role in the utility space, I am constantly working on my personal development and have multiple certifications through Codecademy. I have had exposure to most languages and continue growing my understanding in pursuit of a career in the technology space. In my undergraduate career, I used C++ to create programs capable of sending encrypted files between computers, and programmed an Arduino board sewn into a pair sneakers using conductive thread to wirelessly display health metrics via Bluetooth to a smartphone.
 Through my career as a Reliability Analytics Engineer at National Grid, I have developed skills that have made me a valuable asset to my team and I continue to look for new ways to advance our team further. Having to have spent the majority of my career in a pandemic, I had to learn how to truly navigate through ambiguity and become self-reliant while still being a team member and sharing my best practices. When I first entered National Grid, many of our day-to-day tasks involved running SQL scripts that had to be constantly adjusted. This quickly changed when I taught myself how to write scripts and automated common data requests through workbooks with embedded database requests. With the automated workbooks, managers and VPs have been able to efficiently pull data on the fly without having to ask data engineers for datasets with specific parameters. This is only one of the many accomplishments I have had in the past four years that also include running monthly meetings, automating weekly emails, graduating from a Master’s program in Electrical Engineering with a Advanced Certificate in Power Systems Engineering, and maintaining a database.
 With the tools and knowledge that I now hold, I wish to reconnect with my passions of technology within the consumer space. Having a background in Electrical and Computer Engineering, I am confident in my potential to not only quickly adapt to new roles but make an impact worthy of calling me a teammate.
